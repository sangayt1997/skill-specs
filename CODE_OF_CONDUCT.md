# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give constructive, technically grounded feedback;
- welcome questions and differing perspectives;
- focus criticism on ideas and contributions, not people;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information; and
- accept maintainer decisions made under the repository's documented standards.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, trolling, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. They may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when proportionate to the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, [@sangayt1997](https://github.com/sangayt1997). Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. When appropriate, GitHub's abuse-reporting tools may also be used.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible, and avoid conflicts of interest when handling an incident.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
