# Governance

## Project Model

`skill-specs` is currently maintainer-led. The repository owner, [@sangayt1997](https://github.com/sangayt1997), is the initial maintainer and final decision-maker.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers:

- review issues and pull requests consistently;
- protect the repository's scope and quality standards;
- explain material rejection or revision requests;
- manage releases, repository settings, and security reports; and
- enforce the Code of Conduct.

Contributors are responsible for the accuracy, licensing, and quality of their submissions and for responding constructively to review feedback.

## Decision Process

Routine changes are decided through pull-request review against the published specification and quality standard. Significant changes—such as new categories, overlapping skill scopes, format changes, or governance changes—should begin with a proposal issue so trade-offs can be discussed before implementation.

Maintainers may reject changes that are out of scope, duplicative, unsafe, insufficiently actionable, or too costly to maintain. Approval of one contribution does not guarantee acceptance of similar future changes.

## Becoming a Maintainer

As the community grows, sustained contributors may be invited to maintain specific domains or the repository. Relevant factors include technical judgment, review quality, reliability, respectful participation, and a demonstrated understanding of project standards.

Governance changes must be proposed through a pull request and approved by the current repository owner or the designated maintainer team.
